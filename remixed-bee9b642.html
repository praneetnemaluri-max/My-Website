About

A bit about me

I'm a data analyst with a background spanning fintech, banking, and academic research. Before my masters I spent nearly a year at Finera — a payments fintech — building the dashboards and data models used by real investors for performance reporting. That experience showed me that good analysis is only valuable when it drives a decision.

My MSc at University of Galway has deepened my technical foundation across machine learning, statistical modelling, and data visualisation. I'm most drawn to roles where data sits close to commercial decisions — retention, risk, growth, or operations analytics.

Outside of academics I serve as Secretary of the Eastern European Society and Vice Health & Safety Manager of the Arts Society — both roles I actively sought out because I like contributing to a team.
